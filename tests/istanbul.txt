Istanbul is a beautiful city bridging two continents, Europe and Asia, separated by the scenic Bosphorus Strait. With its rich history spanning empires like the Roman, Byzantine, and Ottoman, the metropolis boasts stunning architecture such as the Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque, and Topkapi Palace. Millions of international visitors every year come to experience its vibrant culture, bustling bazaars like the Grand Bazaar, and world-renowned culinary heritage.
